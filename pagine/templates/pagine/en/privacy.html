Coperni is a public, free site: no registration, no advertising and no profiling, and we do not ask for cookie consent because we only use the cookies needed for the site to work. Below you find in detail which data is involved when you use it.

Data controller

Marco Pavanelli (azuki-beans). For any request about your data write to privacy@azukibeans.dev.

Cookies and data stored in the browser

Only technical cookies, which require no consent. None is shared with third parties.

NamePurposeDuration
centro (cookie)the last place you looked at, to start again from there1 year
django_language (cookie)your language, if you pick it from the menu1 year
coperni.lato, coperni.resa, coperni.sfondo (browser storage)map preferences: area, style, backgrounduntil you delete them

If you use the ⌖ "my position" button, the browser asks for your permission and rounds the coordinates to about 1 km before sending them: your exact position never leaves your device. The rounded coordinates end up in the page address and in the centro cookie.

Data collected by the server

Like any website, the server receives your IP address, browser type, the requested page (with the place or coordinates) and the referring page. This data goes into the technical logs of Google Cloud (Cloud Run, data centre in Belgium), is used only to run the site and fix problems, and is deleted automatically after 30 days.
